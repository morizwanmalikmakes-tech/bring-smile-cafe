OUR STORY & ETHOS

Where Good Taste Brings Genuine Smiles

Located right in the bustling heart of Budh Bazar, Opposite Ajanta Hotel, Bring Smile Cafe was born with a simple promise: to serve delicious comfort food and freshly brewed coffee in a peaceful, welcoming atmosphere.

Whether you want a quick steaming kulhad chai between shopping trips, a romantic evening over cold coffee and pizza, or a quiet corner with high-speed WiFi to work on your laptop, our doors are open 7 days a week.
